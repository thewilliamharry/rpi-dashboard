<objective>
Close the three remaining items round 5 named against background-job health: pin the `job_outcome_unrecorded` floor against external facts instead of against itself, leave durable evidence for a compound startup failure whenever a bounded retry of the failed write succeeds, and stop reporting a busy discovery lock or a contended uptime lock as a fabricated `Background job failed` card.

Purpose: `03-VERIFICATION.md` round 5 reproduced that mutating `UNRECORDED_OUTCOME_FLOOR_SECONDS` to 30 -- below `connect_db`'s own 30-second lock waits and far below discovery's 180-second timeout -- leaves the entire suite byte-identical (296 passed / 413 subtests, unchanged). This is the same shape of hole that let three earlier rounds pass green: a test that derives its own expected value from the constant under test cannot fail for any value of it. Separately, `03-17-REVIEW.md` WR-03 and round 5's own reproduction both confirm a compound startup failure -- the work failed and the write that would record that failure also failed -- still leaves `background_job_health` at `state='running'` after the condition re-raises, so `/advanced` names nothing about it; round-4 `missing` 6 explicitly permitted "re-raise, OR surface it durably", and 03-17 chose re-raise only. And `deferred-items.md` row 8 records two further `return False` sites -- `worker_process_scan_requests`'s discovery-busy branch and `_legacy_do_uptime_check`'s lock-contention branch -- that `dispatch_callback` still maps to a fabricated `job_failed` card, deliberately deferred by round 5 with the verifier's own judgment recorded against them: "another run already owns this work" is not a fault.

Output: The floor's value is enforced by a test that would fail if it regressed below the lock waits or the discovery timeout it is supposed to sit above. A compound startup failure leaves a durable `failed` row for the /advanced workspace whenever the bounded retry succeeds, while continuing to escape `run_worker` loudly either way. A busy discovery claim and a contended uptime probe both record `succeeded`, not a fabricated failure, closing the last two counts of the 03-08 honesty prohibition.
</objective>
