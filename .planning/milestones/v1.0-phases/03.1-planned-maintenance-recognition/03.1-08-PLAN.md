<objective>
Expand outward to MNT-02's operator half: show the candidate window the detector found, in the one
place the operator can act on it, in a treatment that reads unmistakably as a proposal.

The detector landed in plan 03.1-06 and already withholds a proposal that duplicates a confirmed
window. This plan renders what survives: a dashed, muted card above the confirmed list, stating what
Beacon actually observed, with exactly two ways forward and no way to permanently silence it —
because D-12 makes closing the modal the honest equivalent of "not now".

Purpose: keep the suggestion inert and secondary while making it immediately actionable.

Output: the card's markup, its colour-free proposed-state styling, the render function, and the
Confirm and Adjust handlers that both route through the existing window-row builder.
</objective>
