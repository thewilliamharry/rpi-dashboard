<objective>
`Settings.maintenance_default_grace_minutes` is parsed, validated, defaulted, and tested — and read by
nothing. Setting `MAINTENANCE_DEFAULT_GRACE_MINUTES=45` changes the dataclass field and nothing else:
no server-side consumer exists in `app.py`, `beacon/web.py`, `beacon/maintenance.py` or
`beacon/repositories.py`, and no endpoint emits it. The editor's grace prefill comes from an
independent hardcoded literal at `dashboard/app.js:411`, consumed at `app.js:497`, `632` and `645`.
The only Python readers are two assertions at `tests/test_maintenance_windows.py:667-675` that assert
the PARSE, which is why they have stayed green for the whole life of a knob that does nothing.

**The decision, recorded here because the gap demands a decision before an implementation: wire it,
do not delete it.** D-04 requires that the grace field be "prefilled with a sensible default the
operator can change", and 03.1-CONTEXT.md's Claude's Discretion section assigns "the default prefill
value for the grace-period field (D-04)" to planning. A named `Settings` field is the correct home
for a value D-04 obliges us to make sensible and adjustable; deleting the field would remove the
operator's only lever over it and hardcode a product decision into a browser asset. So the field
stays and acquires the consumer it never had.

**The transport, also a decision worth recording rather than leaving silent.** The value rides the
existing `GET /api/service-meta/<port>` payload — the payload `loadMaintenanceWindowsForEditor`
already awaits, which already carries `windows` and `suggestion`. It does NOT ride `/api/config`:
grep finds exactly two references to that route in the whole repository, the route definition and
one assertion in `tests/test_api_and_auth.py`, with no client consumer anywhere. Publishing a
setting on an endpoint nothing reads would reproduce this very defect one level further out. D-01 is
preserved either way: this is a read-side field on a route that already exists, not a new surface.

Purpose: make a configuration knob mean something, and prove it with assertions on observable
behaviour rather than on the parser — the shape that let this rot in the first place.

Output: a default grace that travels from the environment to the editor's grace input, three browser
regressions covering all three client sites, and two server regressions covering the configured and
the malformed value.
</objective>
