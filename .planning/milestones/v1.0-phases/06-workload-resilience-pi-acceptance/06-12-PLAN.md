<objective>
Find out where `/api/services`'s remaining 289 milliseconds go.

Purpose: `06-08` removed the O(buckets x intervals) rescan and cut the route's cost 8.7x (2504.6ms to
289.0ms p50 at concurrency 1 on real Pi hardware), but predicted "tens of milliseconds" and did not
get there. 289.0ms is 58% of the route's own 500ms budget consumed with zero contention, and
`06-VERIFICATION.md` records that residual as un-attributed — nobody yet knows which sub-computation
owns it. `D-DEBT-06-01` names two suspects by argument (the per-service `_uptime_summary` sweep and
the `attributed_downtime_seconds` / offline-interval reconstruction, both still proportional to stored
check count) but the phase has never measured which. `06-13` has to choose a fix path, and choosing
before this profile exists would be guessing.

This plan is the other half of the measurement slice this gap-closure round leads with. It produces
evidence and changes no product behaviour.

by construction — this plan writes no line of `dashboard/app.py` and touches no lock call site.

`06-01`/`06-02`/`06-03` and is untouched by this round.

with their growth ratios, and a regression pinning the profiler.

dataset sizes and shrinks to insignificance at the real one. The shape gate therefore asserts that the
