<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| raw evidence -> aggregate/deletion transaction | Source observations may become deletable only after verified durable replacement. |
| filesystem measurements -> pressure state | Potentially changing DB/WAL/SHM/free-byte values control historical persistence. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-10 | Tampering | `run_retention_batch` source deletion | critical | mitigate | Use unique bucket keys, savepoint rollback, aggregate read-back verification, and exact source-range deletion in the same caller-owned worker transaction. |
| T-02-11 | Denial of Service | retention write/WAL growth | high | mitigate | Process at most 32 ordered buckets per callback, keep one short transaction, and account for main DB, WAL, and SHM bytes. |
| T-02-12 | Denial of Service | storage exhaustion | high | mitigate | Enforce allocation/free-space thresholds, preserve a 64 MiB backlog reserve, suspend only historical writes, and recover through 75% hysteresis. |
| T-02-13 | Repudiation | retry outcomes | medium | mitigate | Persist job state, attempt count, due time, and safe error class under one unique stream/bucket identity. |
</threat_model>
