**The contradiction, stated plainly.** The report's machine-readable gap frontmatter specifies the end state as "DIA-01, DIA-02, UX-02 checked; TEL-06, DIA-03, DIA-08 unchecked". **Resolved in favour of the frontmatter**, for three independent reasons: the frontmatter `missing` list is the report's authoritative statement of required end state and the narrative is a summary of capability satisfaction, which is a different axis from record status; an open gap exists against a clause each of TEL-06's and DIA-08's own requirement text enumerates; and the 03-10 prohibition forbids recording completion on anything less than independent verification.

**The consequence the executor must not undo.** Plans 03-11, 03-12 and 03-13 close Gaps A and B. That does **not** promote DIA-03 or TEL-06. Their closure is an implementation claim by this round about its own work; only a re-verification pass may convert it into a recorded completion. Promoting them here would be a textbook repetition of the very prohibition this plan exists to correct.
