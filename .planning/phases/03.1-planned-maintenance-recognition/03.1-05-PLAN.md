editor they already use, where a window is five plain controls and nothing else.

markup, styling, and the vanilla-JavaScript controller that renders stored windows, adds new ones,
disables them without hiding them, removes them behind a two-step inline confirm, and serializes
the whole list into the existing Save.

Purpose: make the window definition legible and editable without introducing a second modal, a
second error surface, a client dependency, or a native confirm dialog.

render and serialize functions, and source-plus-live-DOM regressions.

| T-03.1-20 | Elevation of Privilege | Client-side validation being mistaken for the security boundary | high | mitigate | The browser's field constraints are convenience only; plan 03.1-03's server-side bounds run before any write and are pinned independently by `WindowValidationTests`. |
| T-03.1-21 | Denial of Service | An operator adding enough rows to make the modal unusable | low | accept | The list scrolls internally past four rows and the server caps the per-service count; an unbounded count is D-05's explicit intent and the cap is the backstop. |
| T-03.1-22 | Repudiation | An accidental single click destroying an operator's window configuration | medium | mitigate | Removal is a two-step inline confirm on the row and nothing leaves storage until the modal's existing Save is used; pinned by `test_removing_a_window_requires_two_activations`. |
