<objective>
Expand outward to the main dashboard: make an expected restart look calm without looking resolved,
make a suppressed night impossible to mistake for a quiet one, and make an overrun look exactly as
bad as it is.

These three surfaces share one discipline established over nine verification rounds in Phase 3: a
displayed state is derived from the durable row it describes and discloses its derivation. The card
never claims the service is fine — it says offline-and-expected, keeps the true down-since line, and
puts the derivation in reach. The events feed never claims a night was uneventful — it states the
hidden count in the control's own label. The overrun entry never softens.

Purpose: satisfy MNT-03's presentation half and MNT-04's visibility half on the surface the operator
actually looks at.

Output: the card state swap and its disclosure, the reveal control and Expected pill, the overrun
entry, their stylesheet rules, and source-plus-live-DOM regressions.
</objective>
