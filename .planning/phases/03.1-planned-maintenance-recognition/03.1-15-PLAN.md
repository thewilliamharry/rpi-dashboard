First, the behavioural half of 03.1-REVIEW.md WR-02. Plan 03.1-14 fixes the two migration sites
structurally, but the only existing close spy,
`test_the_pending_work_check_closes_every_connection_it_opens`, runs against an already-migrated

`_apply_pending_migrations` and never reaches `create_verified_backup`. That is exactly why both
sites went untested through three rounds while a test with "closes every connection it opens" in its
name sat green above them.

Second, 03.1-REVIEW.md WR-01. `run_migrations`'s contention retry loop calls
`exclusive_database_maintenance(database, lock_timeout_seconds)` on every attempt with the full,
unreduced timeout, and only consults `contention_deadline` AFTER `MaintenanceBusy` has been raised.
An attempt that starts a millisecond before the deadline can still block inside `_acquire_lock` for
a further full `lock_timeout_seconds`. With the shipped defaults that makes worst-case wall clock
about 270 seconds against a `CONTENTION_BUDGET_SECONDS = 240` whose own comment derives 240 as a
ceiling — four worst-case ordinary connections at sixty seconds, twenty-four healthcheck cycles at
ten. The comment is only true if the budget is hard. Today it is not.

are, after plan 03.1-14 and this plan's first two tasks, all genuinely satisfied — but nothing on
disk says so in terms the next verifier can confirm rather than re-derive. 03.1-VERIFICATION.md's
frontmatter `gaps:` block is the contract: one failed truth, one artifact, two `missing:` items.
This plan answers those two items by name, with the runnable check that evidences each, and records
the split residual as its own gap so the closure is auditable rather than asserted.
