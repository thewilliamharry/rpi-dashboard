## Two reasoned scope decisions this round

An unexamined silence on either of these is exactly what let earlier rounds of
this phase re-open closed ground; both are recorded as reasoned decisions
instead:

- **`EXPIRE_DAYS` was deliberately NOT capped in `dashboard/beacon/config.py`.**

  trading the gap this round closes for an equivalent one, just moved. The

  in `dashboard/beacon/maintenance.py` and here. Confirmed:
