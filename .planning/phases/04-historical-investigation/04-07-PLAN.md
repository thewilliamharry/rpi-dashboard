<objective>
Build the Incidents section: a filterable list where one row is one grouped down-to-recovered
episode, unresolved outages say so rather than being closed against a clock nobody observed,
overrun outages carry both durable timestamps split at grace expiry, maintenance-suppressed
evidence is shown and tagged rather than hidden, and choosing a row focuses the whole
investigation on that service and window.

Purpose: `/advanced` is the surface an operator opens precisely because they want everything
(D-13), and an incident row and the availability figure for the same service must never disagree
about how long the service was down (D-14).
Output: the `Incidents` nav entry and section; the shared range header relocation; the filter
form and matching count; the incident row anatomy; `focusIncident`.
</objective>
