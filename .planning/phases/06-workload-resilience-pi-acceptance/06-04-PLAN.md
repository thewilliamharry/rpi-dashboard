<objective>
Take the hourly cleanup pass off the thread that Beacon's heartbeat and metric sampling share, and
prove — with the product's own freshness classifier as the oracle — that essential sampling holds its
cadence while every best-effort job runs.

Purpose: `worker_main.py:79-95` assigns J1 (heartbeat, 5s), J2 (metric sampling, 5s) **and** J8
(hourly cleanup, `misfire_grace_time=300`) to the single-thread `'metrics'` executor. A slow cleanup
pass — telemetry retention batches, gap detection, and now the thumbnail reap added in 06-02 — sits
directly in front of the sampling that every freshness surface in the product depends on. This is the
one concrete, verified contention risk `06-RESEARCH.md` identified for OPS-01.

Output: a `'cleanup'` executor lane, J8 moved onto it, and the `cadence_under_contention` test
`06-VALIDATION.md` names for OPS-01.

Note on ordering: this plan depends on 06-03 only because both append to
`tests/test_workload_resilience.py`. There is no logical dependency between bounded preview retry and
executor lanes; the serialization exists to keep same-wave plans free of shared writes.
</objective>
