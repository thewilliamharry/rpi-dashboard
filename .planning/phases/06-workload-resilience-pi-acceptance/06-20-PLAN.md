<plan_review>

**Clauses in this plan that mandate a MECHANISM rather than an outcome, why, and what makes each wrong:**

1. **Task 1 specifies exactly which statements move and which stay, rather than stating "narrow the
   lock".** *Why a mechanism:* the difference between a safe narrowing and `T-06-24`'s threat is
   precisely which statements move. "Narrow the critical section" is satisfiable by moving a
   `conn.execute` out, which is the defect, not the fix. *What would make it wrong:* if a statement
   named as safe to move turns out to touch the connection. `06-19`'s
   `test_no_database_access_escapes_the_db_lock` is the falsifier and it runs in this plan's verify
   command — if the instruction is wrong, that test fails rather than the wrong edit shipping green.

2. **Task 1 requires materializing rows into plain dicts inside the lock rather than relying on
   `sqlite3.Row` surviving connection close.** *Why a mechanism:* the outcome — correct field values
   after the connection closed — is not distinguishable at the response boundary from a Row that
   happens to work today. *What would make it wrong:* nothing about correctness; it costs a small
   amount of work inside the lock. That cost is stated and bounded (one pass over already-fetched
   rows, no additional query), and `06-19` Task 2's `sqlite3.ProgrammingError` mutation is the
   recorded evidence that the hazard is real rather than hypothetical.

**Guards in this plan assert consequences, not shapes:**

| Guard | Asserts | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Golden-output equality | The exact bytes an operator's browser receives | That the route returned `200` |
| Held-region composition, direction A | The measured Python share of the critical section collapsed | That a statement was dedented |
| Held-region composition, direction B | The same instrument reports a high Python share when the moved work runs inside a held region | — |
| `06-19`'s invariant, re-run here | No connection use escaped | That the scope "changed as intended" |

**The rewritten AST pin is knowingly a shape assertion, and is not the load-bearing guard.** It
exists to catch drift — someone quietly moving a computation back inside — and it routes the reader
to `D-DEBT-06-01`. The load-bearing guards are the golden fixture and the composition measurement,
both of which assert consequences. `D-DEBT-06-10`'s lesson is not that shape assertions are
forbidden; it is that a shape assertion must never be the only guard. It is not, here.

**No absolute-millisecond threshold appears anywhere in this plan.** The composition guard's
thresholds are shares of the same run's own hold. `D-DEBT-06-14`'s failure mode is not reachable.

**Timing comparisons use minima with alternating order.** Task 2 direction B is a share, not a
duration comparison. If the implementer adds any before/after duration comparison, it must take the
minimum of at least five alternating-order repetitions per arm and compare minima, never sums —
`06-18` recorded that summed one-sided noise is what made two tests fail 3-in-5 in isolation.
