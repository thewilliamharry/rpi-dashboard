<objective>
Take round 5's second attribution point, then produce acceptance evidence that is admissible.

Two segments, one session, one commit.

**Segment A is the attribution point for `06-22`'s change.** `06-21` measured the narrowing alone.
This measures what `06-22` added on top, with the same instrument, so the two halves of round 5 stay
separately attributed. This phase has spent four rounds learning that a change measured only in
combination cannot be reasoned about; round 4 built the instrument specifically so a reading could be
taken between changes.

**Segment B is the acceptance run, and it must be uninstrumented.** `PROH-OPS-07-11` states plainly
that an instrumented run is never OPS-07 evidence, whichever way its numbers fall, and round 4's own
in-situ overhead check measured the instrument perturbing `/api/services`' control p50 by 23.05% —
outside the 15% band it calibrates against. So the acceptance figures must come from a build with the
flag off, on the same commit as segment A, in the same session.

**This round does not promote OPS-07 even if the run passes.** `PROH-OPS-07-08` scopes promotion to
an independent verification round, and `D-DEBT-06-08`'s self-certification rule says the round that
produces evidence is not the round that certifies it. The artifact states the result and the
requirement stays Pending; `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` is not edited and the artifact asserts the
check that proves it.

**The run may still fail, and the artifact records that as a result rather than a setback.** Four
routes were over budget in round 4. The narrowing addresses the lock-wait component of three of them
and `06-22` addresses the fourth's cost or the topology. Whether that is sufficient is exactly what
this session measures, and the round has been designed so a `false` is as informative as a `true`.

**And the acceptance rotation is where OPS-03's bounded thumbnail store is exercised under load.**
`/api/thumbnail/<port>` and `/api/thumbnail-status` are both in the rotation with their own budgets,
and `/api/thumbnail/<port>` was one of round 4's four over-budget routes. Their behaviour is governed
by `06-CONTEXT.md` D-02's `THUMBNAIL_TTL_DAYS` and `THUMBNAIL_STORE_MAX_BYTES` defaults, which round
5 does not change — segment B measures them as they stand rather than assuming they still hold.

Purpose: `06-VERIFICATION.md` Truth 5's fifth `missing:` item — a hardware acceptance re-run after a
fix chosen against the diagnostic measurements.
Output: `06-ACCEPTANCE-ROUND5.md`, three attached hardware reports, and updated debt entries.
</objective>

<plan_review>

**Clauses in this plan that mandate a MECHANISM, why, and what makes each wrong:**

1. **Segment B must run on an uninstrumented build, on the same commit as segment A.** *Why:* the
   outcome — admissible acceptance evidence — is defined by `PROH-OPS-07-02` and `PROH-OPS-07-11` in
   terms of how the run was produced, not what it reported. There is no way to read admissibility off
   a number. *What would make it wrong:* running segment B on a different commit, which would make
   its figures un-attributable to `06-20` and `06-22`. The acceptance criteria require one SHA
   recorded once and asserted identical across both segments, and require the diagnostic endpoint to
   return 404 before segment B starts.

2. **Three `service_checks` counts, not one.** *Why:* `D-DEBT-06-14`'s failure was an absolute figure
   compared across a dataset that had grown 2.24x. A single count taken at the start cannot show
   growth across a session that runs three passes over roughly twenty-five minutes. *What would make
   it wrong:* nothing; it costs three queries and is the difference between a comparison and a
   coincidence.

3. **`assertions.cadence` must be recorded on all three passes, and OPS-01's status stated
   explicitly.** *Why:* cadence has held on three hardware runs, it is a phase success criterion, and
   both of round 5's changes touch request-path code that shares a machine with the worker. An
   unstated cadence result reads as an absent one. *What would make it wrong:* nothing — this is a
   recording requirement, and a regression here is a stop-and-report.

**Every comparison in the artifact is relational or explicitly dataset-scaled.** Each route's p95 is
stated against its own unchanged budget — a same-run comparison, and the budgets are untouched
(`PROH-OPS-07-01`). Every cross-round absolute figure carries both rounds' row counts. Utilisation is
dimensionless. The narrowing-outcome and attribution verdicts are computed from shares and ratios by
`06-19`'s functions, not composed in prose.

**Guards assert consequences.** `overall_passed` and `failure_reasons` come from the harness's own
oracles, which this plan does not touch. The artifact's job is to transcribe them and name the field
each figure came from — the requirement round 4's artifact adopted after `06-ACCEPTANCE-ROUND3.md`'s
GIL reading had to be withdrawn for drawing a conclusion its own sentence contradicted.

**Timing comparisons.** The absolute per-request totals from `requests[*]` are transcribed as
evidence, never compared as a threshold. Nothing in this plan gates on a summed duration.

</plan_review>
