<plan_review>

**Clauses in this plan that mandate a MECHANISM, why, and what makes each wrong:**

1. **The re-audit must run as `/gsd-secure-phase 06`, not as a hand-written review.** *Why:*
   `PROH-OPS-04-05` names that command specifically, and its value is that it is an independent pass
   over the phase's threat register rather than the same reasoning that produced the change. A
   hand-written review by the agent that made the change is the shape `D-DEBT-06-10` records four
   gates falling into — each confirming a requirement was met without questioning whether it was
   right. *What would make it wrong:* if the command's output does not actually re-derive
   `T-06-24`'s evidence. Task 1's acceptance criteria require the pasted output to be checked for
   `T-06-24` by name; if it does not appear, that is recorded as an incomplete re-audit rather than
   waved through.

2. **Every debt entry must state its evidence, and none may be closed on an outstanding
   prerequisite.** *Why:* the outcome — a record a cold reader can trust — is not observable from
   entry statuses alone, because a status is exactly the thing a rushed close gets wrong. *What
   would make it wrong:* an entry whose closure condition was reworded to match what happened. Task 2
   requires each closed entry to quote its own previously-stated closure condition before declaring
   it met.

**Guards assert consequences, not shapes.** This plan's checkable outputs are the re-audit's threat
count at or above the blocking severity, the presence of `T-06-24` in its output, and per-entry
closure conditions quoted verbatim from their own prior text. None is "a section exists".

**No threshold in this plan is a duration or an absolute measurement.** Nothing here measures.

**No new prohibition is minted.** Round 5's four are `PROH-OPS-04-06` (`06-20`), `PROH-OPS-07-13`
(`06-19`) and `PROH-OPS-07-14` (`06-22`); this plan records them in one place rather than adding to
them.
