<objective>
Establish whether `06-25` actually made the route cheaper on this host, before any Pi time is spent.

**Why this is a separate plan and not a paragraph in `06-25`.** Five rounds of this phase have gone
to hardware on the strength of a reasoned expectation. Round 5 landed two changes, went to the Pi,
and measured `/api/services` **68.4% worse**. The cheapest available guard against a sixth
repetition is a local before/after on a fixed dataset, taken between the change and the hardware
round. It costs minutes.

**Why the instrument gets less useful exactly here, and what to do about it.**
`tests/services_route_profile.py` attributes cost with `cProfile`, which measures Python calls.
`06-25` moved the dominant Python bucket (`uptime_sweep`, 43.727%) into a SQLite aggregation
executed in C. cProfile will report that bucket at roughly zero **whether the work got cheaper or
more expensive**, because the SQL time lands inside `sql_execute`'s builtin entry. So the share
table cannot answer the question this plan exists to answer. `wall_ms_unprofiled` — the profiler's
own uninstrumented wall measurement, already reported alongside `wall_ms_profiled` for precisely
this reason — is the headline, and the share table is context.

**What a good result looks like, stated before the measurement.** `06-PROFILE-2.md` measured
`wall_ms_unprofiled` at **58.533ms** on this host at seed `20260902`, 8 services, 8 days, 5 repeats.
If `uptime_sweep`'s real share is anywhere near its measured 43.727%, the after figure should fall
materially below that. If it does not fall at all, option C's premise is refuted on this host and
`06-27` must not be run as planned.

**What this plan may not conclude.** Nothing about Pi latency (`PROH-OPS-07-09`) and nothing about
whether OPS-07 passes. A dev-host wall-time delta is a directional signal about a cost model, not a
prediction of a p95 under concurrency 3 on a Pi 5.

Purpose: make option C's cost claim falsifiable locally before it is expensive to falsify.
Output: `06-PROFILE-3.md`, a profiler that still attributes what it measures, and one honest guard.
</objective>
