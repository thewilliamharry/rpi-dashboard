<objective>
Take the 168-bucket strip off the expensive path by feeding its existing Python producer the ~0.3%
of points that actually carry information, and revert `06-25`'s SQL wiring in the same commit.

**The premise this round was handed is refuted, and that refutation is the first deliverable.**
The scoping in `06-GUARD-DECISION.md` §8 assumes `service_rollups` already stores the strip's 168
hourly buckets. It does not, and cannot. Measured on the profiled 8-service/8-day shape with the
worker's own `run_retention_batch` driven to convergence (73 batches, 2,304 buckets rolled):
**`service_rollups` holds 0 rows at `bucket_seconds=3600`, and 0 rows of any tier inside the strip
window.** The 2,304 rows that exist are all at `bucket_seconds=300` and all aged 7.00-8.00 days —
outside the window entirely. `service_checks` holds 22,247 rows covering exactly `[now-7d, now]`.
The reason is structural: `_raw_candidates` only admits buckets older than `raw_days` (7 days), and
`UPTIME_WINDOW_SECONDS` is also 7 days, so the rollup ladder *begins where the strip ends*; the
3600s tier additionally requires age past `five_minute_days` (30 days). **This project already knew
this** — `D-DEBT-06-21` recorded the identical finding on 2026-09-05 ("0 buckets inside the window,
2016 outside"). Round 7 re-proposed a path round 6 refuted. `PROH-OPS-07-29`.

**And the rollup could not work even if it were populated.** `604800 == 168 * 3600`, so
`start ≡ now (mod 3600)` and every rendered boundary sits at offset `now % 3600` from the epoch-hour
grid. Not "two partial edge buckets": measured across five random `now` values, **168 of 168 buckets
straddle an epoch hour**, and apportioning hour-aligned totals into them changes **150-157 of the 168
rendered values**, worst absolute error **0.461** on a 3-decimal fraction. An hour-aligned rollup is
a rendered-contract change — exactly what `PROH-OPS-07-15` forbids and what made option C's shape
wrong in `06-PREMISE-C.md` mismatch #1.

**What is actually wrong is much smaller, and nobody looked at it.** `_legacy_uptime_summary` builds
one interval per *check*, not per *state change*. At the profiled shape that is 25,278 points
producing ~25,278 intervals whose information content is **72 points**. Overlap sums are additive
over a contiguous partition, so merging adjacent same-state intervals is exactly equal — not
approximately. Feeding the producer only state-change points is therefore output-identical by
construction, needs no new table, no worker job, no migration, no schema change, and no SQL.

**Pre-registered, before this plan was written (`PROH-OPS-07-26`).** Built on a worktree at HEAD with
option C's route wiring reverted and the reduction applied — the exact shape this plan ships:

| build | mean `wall_ms_unprofiled` | spread |
|---|---|---|
| pre-`06-25` bar (`06-PROFILE-3.md`) | 56.820ms | 0.230ms |
| this plan's shape, planner-measured | **36.943ms** | 1.222ms |

`-19.877ms`, **-34.98%** against the bar. The same bench reproduced the `79e051e` baseline at
57.012ms — within 0.34% of `06-PROFILE-3.md`'s 56.820ms — so the harness and host are consistent
with the reference. Component-level: `uptime_sweep` falls from 236.666ms to 12.291ms tottime
(**19.3x**); every other bucket is flat within noise. **Projection for `06-32`'s measurement: ~37ms,
band 34-42ms, projected verdict PASS.** Unlike rounds 1-6 this is a *reproduction of a measurement
already taken on the shipping shape*, not an extrapolation from a component bench — which is why the
band is narrow. If `06-32` measures at or above 56.820ms, that is a FAILURE with a stop-and-revert
branch, and this projection is refuted and recorded as refuted.

The full suite was run on that same worktree: **986 passed, 593 subtests passed, 2 failed** — and
both failures are the mechanical lock re-pins Task 2 performs, nothing else. The three golden
fixtures byte-matched unregenerated.

Purpose: end OPS-07's cost problem at the layer where it actually lives, without building
infrastructure whose premise is refuted and whose output would break the rendered contract.
Output: one production commit, three new guards, a realigned lock audit.
</objective>

    raises. The planner measured this: on the randomized case space it diverges on 39 of 1,802 cases,
    and on the three-row case `[(now-3000, 0), (now-2000, 0), (now-100, None)]` the naive form keeps

    interval per point, and because `cursor` only advances, those intervals form a contiguous,
